# Lithium Supplier Scout Agent

## When to use
Use this skill to find and evaluate new lithium suppliers for battery-grade materials, recycled lithium, or lithium chemicals to onboard to the LithiumBuy marketplace.

## Instructions
1. Search supplier databases, industry directories, and trade registries for lithium suppliers
2. Filter by material type (lithium carbonate, hydroxide, spodumene, recycled cells)
3. Scrape or request supplier profiles: capacity, certifications, MOQ, pricing tiers
4. Verify certifications (ISO 9001, battery grade purity standards, ESG ratings)
5. Score suppliers on reliability, geographic proximity, and sustainability metrics
6. Generate ranked supplier shortlist with contact info and qualification notes
7. Draft outreach templates for supplier onboarding to LithiumBuy

## Environment
- Runtime: python-3.12
- Trigger: Manual
- Category: Lithium & Supply Chain Agents

## Examples
- Find battery-grade lithium carbonate suppliers in South America
- Scout recycled lithium suppliers with ESG certification in North America
- Identify alternative suppliers when primary source has capacity issues
